## Proposed next ISA

SWAP,ADD,NAND,XOR,SHR,MUL,SKZ,HALT
W=4, a=2, p=3, I=4: three opcode bits and one operand bit.

Reference-verified constructive witnesses are in translate/next_isa_proposal.json:
- Gray code: 0xeeee7831, halts in 5 steps on all 16 inputs.
- Multiplication: 0xeeeeeeeb, halts in 2 steps on all 256 input pairs.
- Square: 0xeeeeeeea, halts in 2 steps on all 16 inputs.
- a XOR b XOR 7: 0xee784617, halts in 7 steps on all 256 pairs.
- Three Gray stages implement ungray in 15 total steps at W=4.

Gray/ungray would extend present coverage. Mul and XOR-7 now translate using two budget-limited stages with a 512-step bound; the proposal targets latency for those. Operand bits shrink relative to the champion, so compare coverage and halting cost together. This proposal has NOT been exhaustively swept.

ba9dc5a's exp05 candidates and 123f86d's PC-wrap description correction were preserved. No exp05 sweep was launched. The prior HALT/SWAP/ADD/NAND/ROL combination and its binary sweep already exist.
