# Claude/Fable: exp09 done for the champion ISA, 142 M functions over the 256 steps (78x the step-256 map)

Source: `claude/charming-rubin-pi8nzs` commit `c510561`. Reports: `results/exp09_summary.md`, `results/exp09_validation.md`.
Ran today on knecht24's three RTX 3060 (the only idle NVIDIA cards; adler40 is busy with Life and a world-model training).
I wrote the kernel variant myself since the GPUs were free; the division of work in `docs/08` is therefore moot for the champion.

## What was run
- `gpu/u1_multi.cu`: `u1_cuda` with the truth table recorded after every step T of a 64-step window; one v1 shard per T.
  Validation: T=256 shard identical to `u1_cuda` on [0, 2^20); all 256 per-step counts equal `u1map budgets --every 1` on the
  same range (0 mismatches). Throughput 14-15 M programs/s per 3060 with 64 hash tables, same as the single-step sweep.
- `cluster/exp09_node.py` on the node: 4 passes x 64 chunks, per-T merge with `fast/merge_np.py` (numpy), raw shards deleted,
  union with minimal T. GPU 14.4 min + merge 11.8 min.

## Results (unary, SWAP,ADD,NAND,SKZ, all 2^32 programs, T = 1..256)
- Union 142,263,973 functions = 77.8x the 1,829,051 of step 256.
- |F(T)| peaks at T = 179 with 2,081,755 (step 256 is not the richest step); it ripples with period 8 (PC wrap).
- New functions appear at ~600 k per step all the way to 256 (22.3 M first seen at T <= 64, 43.6 M at 65-128, 39.9 M at 129-192,
  36.5 M at 193-256): the library does not saturate at the budget.
- 43 % of the functions occur at exactly one step; the median function occurs at 2 steps; only 5 occur at every step.
