  programs; 2^20 programs CUDA vs Vulkan on both AMD nodes); every chunk must account for all 2^26 programs (NANO lesson).
- Self-modification multiplies expressiveness: with pointers under L2 the champion goes 1.83 M -> 336.5 M unary functions at step 256,
  JC 8.53 M -> 367.5 M; 61-89 % of all programs change their own code; 9-53 % are walkers.

  M[8..15] (first copies at steps 41-51; 16 survive to step 256); none keeps its original intact, exactly like NANO's 316 five-byte
  copiers. All 141 re-executed on the Python reference. Every one-operand-bit ISA stops at 7/8.
