# Claude/Fable: exp10 (512-step clock), the first 512-step Life world, a learned world model, showcase videos

Source: `claude/charming-rubin-pi8nzs` commit `10ad1a2`. All GPU work today ran on adler40 and knecht24; the AMD cards stayed idle.

## exp10: steps 257..512 of every champion program (`results/exp10_w4_o2_add_summary.md`)
- Union over T = 1..512: 267,132,154 functions (146x the step-256 map); 124.9 M of them first appear beyond step 256.
- Richest single step: T = 355 (2,095,387). New functions per step fall slowly from 570 k around 256 to 450 k at 512; a linear
  extrapolation ends near step 1,100 at roughly 400 M functions, so the library is large but finite in practice.
- The late functions are the least persistent (48 % exist at one step only, none at 256+ steps); only two catalog names first appear
  beyond 256 (`x >> 1` at T = 352, `x < 10` at T = 409). The persistent core (184 k functions at 256+ steps) is all before 256.
- Validation: steps 257..512 re-executed on the Python reference for sampled witnesses and programs (384/384); steps <= 256 unchanged.
- Cost: 30 min GPU on three RTX 3060 + 13 min merge. Data on knecht24 `results/exp09/w4_o2_add/` (T001..T512.npy 24 GB, union_1-512.npy 4.3 GB).
- Usable now: `sim/machine.py run(max_steps=T)`, `u1map` `run_budget`, Life engines `--max-steps`; the union file gives (key, minT, program, persistence).

## Life on the 512-step clock (`results/life/ANALYSIS.md`, run champ512_s1)
- `u1life_cuda` and the CPU reference take `--max-steps` (bit-identical at 512 on a 64^2 test; Vulkan not ported).
- Income 20 at 512 steps goes extinct within 1,000 ticks (cost 32 > income); income 36 (same net +4 as 20 at 256) runs stably:
  87 % fill, 89 k genomes, and the strongest dominance of any run so far (top genome 20 %, top two 32 %, both reproduce on 38 % of
  inputs with neighbour-dependent outcomes). 197 ticks/s on the RTX 4080.

## Learned world model (`results/life/worldmodel/REPORT.md`)
A 1.4 M-parameter circular conv net trained on the 4090 while the JC world ran on the 4080 (grid pairs every 100 ticks), predicting
the grid one tick ahead. Held-out ticks of the training world: next state 97.6 % (copy baseline 49.6 %), genome 99.3 % (copy 99.05 %),
alive 99.8 %, energy error 1.2/255. Unseen seed-2 world: state 81.5 %, genome 98.6 %. It learned the world rule and most of the
program semantics of the families it saw; cross-world drop = partial memorisation of family behaviour.

## Showcase videos
`cluster/showcase_video.py`: torus + live dashboard (tick, fill, diversity, births, curves drawing themselves) + a soundtrack generated
from the stats (pitch = diversity, loudness = birth rate, click per 10 k ticks). Rendered for jc_s1 and champ512_s1 (340 MB each, on
the workstation; 22 MB plain versions of jc_s1/jc_s2 are in the repo). Frame output: `u1life_cuda --ppm-every k`.
