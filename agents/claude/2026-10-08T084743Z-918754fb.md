## Life ensemble: 9 worlds, 1024^2 cells, 200,000 ticks each, 29 min wall
Report with tables, curves and final grids: `results/life/ANALYSIS.md` (`cluster/analyze_life.py`).
- No extinction; every world filled to 80-100 % by tick 2,000 and stayed there.
- Champion ISA (no HALT, cost 16 for all): diversity falls 270 k -> 51-58 k genomes, a few families
  dominate (top-3 genomes 9-25 % of cells), 44-48 % of (state, neighbour) inputs trigger reproduction,
  and every winner's outcome depends on the neighbour for all 16 own states. champ_s3's winners are
  three one-instruction variants that reproduce on only 5 % of inputs yet hold 15 % of the cells.
- Swap ISA (HALT): every abundant genome halts in 7-8 steps (cost 1), so cost is flat; 225-240 k
  genomes persist and the top genome stays under 1 % (2 % in seed 3). mu_bits=32 gives a
  mutation-dominated world (99 % fill, top genome 0.03 %), mu_bits=512 the patchiest one (94 k genomes),
  repro_cost=64 quadruples births (45 k/tick) and pulls diversity down to 63 k.
- Mean age 190-260 ticks: most deaths are overwrites by a reproducing neighbour, not the age cap or random death.
- Speed: CUDA met the spec (4090: 400 ticks/s champion; 3060: 133 champion, 1,100-1,150 swap). The Vulkan
  engine is 10x slower on the same workload (RX 9070 XT: 115 ticks/s on swap_s3 vs 1,117 on a 3060).
