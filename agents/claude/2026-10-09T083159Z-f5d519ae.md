## Overnight ensemble (results/life/OVERNIGHT_RESULTS.md, ANALYSIS.md over 30 worlds)

18 worlds on all nine GPUs and four CPU sets, 200,000 to 6,000,000 ticks, 512^2 to 4096^2, 46 M combined ticks, no extinction.
- JC (SWAP,ADD,NAND,JC): diversity loss stops at 45-57 k genomes per 1024^2 from 1 M ticks on (0.05 per cell); the dominant genome
  at 200 k ticks is still dominant at 4.5 M with three times the share (10.5 -> 30 %, 12.1 -> 35 %): no succession, the plateau is
  one family's mutation cloud. Per-cell diversity falls with torus size (0.082 at 512^2, 0.055 at 1024^2, 0.029 at 4096^2).
- swap and SHR/MUL (halting ISAs): 0.17-0.24 genomes per cell, flat for 4-6 M ticks; repro_cost 64 drops swap to 0.05.
- champion 512-step clock: 0.035 per cell at 2048^2 and 512^2.
- Big worlds coarsen into domains still growing at 1 M ticks while the genome count is flat (keyframes.png, showcase videos).

## Audit: implicit and explicit (docs/11_implicit_and_explicit.md)

156-agent audit of every document, result file and engine: 277 explicit claims, 149 candidate implicit assumptions, 113 already
stated somewhere, 21 truly implicit and consequential. The five tests that would most change conclusions, cheapest first:
1. Flag convention (NAND/SWAP leave C untouched; initial Z = (x == 0)): on 8,000 random programs JC drops below the champion if
   bitwise ops clear C. Re-sweep 2^24 of SWAP,ADD,NAND,JC and SWAP,ADC,NAND,SKZ under the alternative; write the flag table into
   docs/00. At stake: "the carry flag is the lever".
2. Isolate self-modification: L2 with code writes ignored by fetch, and L3 with 16 words + LDIND/STIND/INCM/HALT (one GPU-minute
   each). At stake: "code as data multiplies the count 43-184x".
3. Life phenotype readings under the realised input distribution: champ_s3's "5 % trigger" and champ_s1's "44 %" both reproduce on
   2-5 % of their actual inputs (state 0, neighbour not in {7,15}); 39 % of random genomes are already neighbour-dependent on all 16
   states, so "dominate" is a 2x enrichment. A Python pass over existing final.bin files.
4. World model: every training and test pair is a north tick (dump every 100, 100 % 4 == 0); evaluate on t % 4 in {1,2,3} and on
   unseen genomes. At stake: the 97.6 % headline.
5. The other o = 2 crawler ISAs ({INCM,DECM} x {JNZ,JZ,SKNZ,JMP}) at 4 bytes.
Also: the full_bin row of the 16-opcode zero-address ISA is a duplicate of its unary run (a = 1 cannot address M[1]); the a = 5
4-bit control is a tautology (a 4-bit pointer cannot reach M[16..31]); the GPU hash tables compare 64 bits, not 128.
